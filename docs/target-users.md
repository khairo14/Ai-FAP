# Target Users

## Primary User Segments (Priority Order)

### 1. Freelancers
**Priority:** Highest

**Characteristics:**
- Irregular income streams
- Multiple clients/projects
- Need to track business expenses
- Self-employed tax considerations

**Pain Points:**
- Difficulty separating personal and business expenses
- Irregular cash flow management
- Tax preparation challenges
- No employer-provided benefits

**How FundVance AI Helps:**
- Categorize business vs. personal expenses
- Track income from multiple sources
- Project-based expense tracking
- Tax-ready expense reports

---

### 2. Young Professionals
**Priority:** High

**Characteristics:**
- Age: 22-35
- Regular salary
- Growing career
- First-time budgeters

**Pain Points:**
- Living paycheck to paycheck
- Student loan debt
- Lifestyle inflation
- Limited financial literacy

**How FundVance AI Helps:**
- Build first budget
- Understand spending patterns
- Set realistic savings goals
- Subscription management

---

### 3. Small Business Owners
**Priority:** Medium-High

**Characteristics:**
- Running micro-businesses
- 1-10 employees
- Need expense oversight
- Budget-conscious

**Pain Points:**
- Business expense tracking
- Cash flow management
- Multiple expense categories
- Financial planning uncertainty

**How FundVance AI Helps:**
- Business expense categorization
- Vendor spending analysis
- Budget allocation recommendations
- Cost reduction insights

---

### 4. Couples Managing Shared Expenses
**Priority:** Medium

**Characteristics:**
- Living together or married
- Shared and individual expenses
- Joint financial goals
- Need transparency

**Pain Points:**
- Splitting bills fairly
- Tracking who paid what
- Shared budget planning
- Financial communication

**How FundVance AI Helps:**
- Shared expense tracking
- Fair split calculations
- Joint goal setting
- Expense visibility for both partners

---
